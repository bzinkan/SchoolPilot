# App icons

Retrieved on November 18, 2026 from the linked services' official asset hosts.
These vendor-provided images identify the corresponding apps. They are bundled
locally so opening the app picker does not contact a favicon service or send
teachers' saved website addresses to a third party.

Google's current Workspace product navigation links the Docs, Slides, Sheets,
Forms, and Drive artwork below. IXL's homepage links its favicon directly;
Quill's homepage links both its 16px favicon and the larger touch icon used here.
Classroom uses Google's official product icon, matching its favicon artwork,
for a sharper result than its 16px favicon. The Classroom bundle uses the same
Classroom icon and keeps its bundle label.

All output files are 64 × 64 PNGs with their original transparency preserved. SVGs were rasterized and larger
PNGs resized with Sharp, with no recoloring, cropping, or recreated artwork.
The marks remain the property of their respective providers.
